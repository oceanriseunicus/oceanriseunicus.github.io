What are AI & Robotics labs?

Government-supported workspaces aimed at fostering innovation among institutes. Students engage in hands-on STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) using cutting-edge tools like 3D printers, robotics kits and IoT devices.


They work on solving real-world problems, building entrepreneurial skills and nurturing creativity.
